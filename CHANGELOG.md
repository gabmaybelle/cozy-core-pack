# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release of the Cozy Core Pack: a cozy, builder-friendly survival pack for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric (Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer), made to run well on an ordinary PC.

- **Cozy Core 0.3.0:** creepers, ghasts, endermen and fire can't break your builds, there are no phantoms, you keep your hotbar when you die, the Hearth Lantern keeps monsters from spawning near your build site, wood goes in the stonecutter at cheap rates, and you can re-dye blocks.
- **Our own mods:** Spawn Gazebo 1.1.1 (you start in a little gazebo with a starter chest), Nightfall Clock 0.2.1, Pocket List 0.1.0 and Not Now, Shield 1.2.0.
- **For building and the world:** Beautify, Ecologics, Biomes O' Plenty, FallingTree, Universal Graves, Traveler's Backpack, Waystones, Carry On, Right Click Harvest and Sit.
- **Finding your way and seeing what's there:** Nature's Compass, Explorer's Compass, Xaero's World Map and Minimap, JEI, Jade, Zoomify, Mouse Tweaks, inventory sorting and the usual tooltips.
- **Looks and speed:** a light shader (MakeUp UltraFast) is on from the start, and Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling and Dynamic FPS keep it smooth.
